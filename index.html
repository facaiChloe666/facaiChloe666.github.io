About Me

I am Feng Chun (冯纯), a researcher in Information Systems. My work examines how AI is changing organizations, labor markets, and the way people work and learn, with a focus on human-AI collaboration, AI agents, and automated experimentation.

My academic background spans economics at South China University of Technology and statistics at Xi'an Jiaotong University. My doctoral studies in Information Systems at the National University of Singapore are planned for 2026–2030.

At the Digital Transformation Center, Cheung Kong Graduate School of Business, I collaborate with Tianshu Sun (Cheung Kong Graduate School of Business), Xiaoning Wang (The University of Texas at Dallas), and Yingjie Zhang (Guanghua School of Management, Peking University) on AI, organizations, labor markets, and social networks.

I am also deeply interested in labor-market questions in the age of AI: which kinds of people will develop an advantage in work and learning, how firms should recruit employees who can create value in complementarity with AI, and how humans can collaborate with AI to produce better outcomes rather than focusing only on improving model capabilities.

Research interests: Human-AI collaboration; AI agents; automated experimentation; AI and labor markets; social networks; natural experiments.
